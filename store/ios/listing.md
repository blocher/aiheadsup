## Description (4000)

Forehead Frenzy is the loud, laugh-out-loud party game where the secret word sits on YOUR forehead and everyone else helps you guess it.

Hold your phone to your forehead. Friends shout clues. Tilt down when you nail it, tip up to pass. Race the clock, rack up points, and fight for the high score.

WHY IT’S A BLAST
• Zero setup — pick a pack and play in seconds
• Perfect for 2+ players at family nights, road trips, and parties
• Big, readable cards designed for forehead play
• Tilt to score (or tap buttons if you prefer)
• High scores and recent rounds saved per deck

HUNDREDS OF READY-TO-PLAY PACKS
Browse themed decks across movies & shows, cities, sports, science, history, food, mythology, and more. Search, filter by category, and jump straight into a 30-, 60-, or 90-second round.

MAKE YOUR OWN WITH AI
Can’t find the perfect topic? Tap “Make a pack with AI” and generate a custom deck about literally anything — Taylor Swift songs, inside jokes, classroom vocab, you name it. On iPhone, on-device AI is available when supported (private & offline). Or add your own Gemini or OpenAI key for cloud generation. Your keys stay on your device; packs stay on your phone.

SPOILER-SAFE PLAY
Series packs (Harry Potter, Star Wars, Indiana Jones, and more) can respect how far you’ve gotten so later twists don’t spoil the fun.

SHARE & IMPORT DECKS
Export AI decks as TOML packages and share them with friends. Import packs others send you and keep the frenzy going.

No account. No ads. Just grab a phone, pick a pack, and get silly.

## App Review notes (optional paste for Review Information)

Forehead Frenzy is a local multiplayer party game. No account or login is required.

Gameplay: pick a deck → Start round → hold phone to forehead (landscape) → friends give clues → tilt down for correct / tilt up to pass (or use on-screen buttons).

AI deck generation (optional feature):
• On supported devices, on-device AI may be available with no API key.
• Cloud AI requires the reviewer to paste their own Gemini or OpenAI API key in Settings → Decks & AI. Keys are stored in the iOS Keychain and never leave the device except as requests to the chosen provider.
• The app ships with many bundled decks, so AI is not required to evaluate core gameplay.

Motion: NSMotionUsageDescription explains tilt scoring. Please allow motion when prompted so tilt controls work; buttons remain available in Settings if motion is denied.

Demo path for reviewers:
1. Open the app and dismiss the how-to overlay (or tap ? anytime).
2. From the library, open any pack (e.g. a Science or Kids pack).
3. Choose 30 or 60 seconds and tap Start.
4. Hold the phone in landscape against your forehead, or use Correct / Pass buttons.
