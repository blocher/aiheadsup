# Age rating questionnaire answers

Suggested answers for App Store Connect’s age rating form. Adjust if your release differs.

## Apple age rating target

**4+** — no unrestricted web, no mature themes, no gambling, no frequent/intense violence.

## In-app controls relevant to rating

- Audience selector when creating AI packs: Kids / Family / Teens+ / Adults — helps keep generated content appropriate for the chosen group.
- No user accounts, chat, or UGC feed between strangers.
- Sharing decks is file-based (export/import), peer-to-peer via the system share sheet.
